# M8 — Offline portfolio risk engine

Pre-M8 commit baseline: master at `0e7c8b84ef540ad1c4774746ed84849893d8ddd4`. M7 received independent OpenCode PASS and is committed. All **90 existing M0–M7 files are tracked** and remain covered by the SHA-256 inventory in ignored `runs/m8-preservation.json`: 82 M0–M6 files and eight committed M7 files. OpenCode's M8 audit returned PASS WITH FINDINGS, safe to commit; M8-F01 updates documentation references only. This implementation preserves M0–M7 and adds the seven M8 files described below.

## Scope and module boundaries

`trading/portfolio.py` owns immutable quotes, explicit linear instrument specifications, Decimal accounting, reservations, simulated positions and append-only accounting events. It reuses existing UTC timestamp validation. The existing `Ledger`/`Halts` are single-instrument, float-based simulator contracts; they are preserved rather than modified or overloaded with multi-asset/FX semantics.

`trading/risk.py` owns immutable policy/cost assumptions, normalized signal proposals, an isolated M7-v1 adapter, sizing, portfolio guards, risk decisions and terminal receipts. It imports M7 only inside the two adapter functions. There is no import from M8 into M0–M7, no M4 dependency and no execution adapter. M9, Freqtrade, Telegram, real accounts and network operations are outside scope.

All calculations are offline. `APPROVED` and `REDUCED` reserve journal capital; neither places an order. `simulate_open` and `simulate_close` record synthetic accounting events only. No paper-trading readiness, empirical safety or profitability is claimed.

## User-selected risk policy and currency

Portfolio defaults are **INR 2,000** and `RiskConfig.risk_fraction=Decimal('.10')`. The initial planned-loss budget is therefore **INR 200**. This is the user's explicitly selected aggressive experimental setting, not a recommendation or a validated safe level. The fraction is configurable and applies to **current equity**, including realized and unrealized P&L, fees and posted funding. It does not apply to position notional, invested margin or starting capital after equity has changed.

BTC/USDT and ETH/USDT marks are in USDT. Callers must supply a timestamped FX `Quote(key='USDT', value=..., ...)` denominated in **account currency per USDT**. No INR/USDT rate is fetched, assumed or silently substituted. Test FX values are synthetic. Account currency is explicit; all cash, budgets, costs, exposure and margins returned by the engine are in that currency. Exchange minimum notional stays in quote currency. Contract quantities are contracts, with an explicit quote-P&L-per-price-unit multiplier; multiplier 1 may represent base-asset quantity. Inverse, options, quanto, multi-collateral and hedged same-symbol books are not supported.

Rates are fractions, not percentages or basis points. Fee and slippage fractions are in [0,1); estimated total horizon funding debit is nonnegative and can exceed one. Expected funding credits never expand a risk budget. Funding estimation has no automatic settlement calendar or forecast; caller-provided horizon assumptions must cover the proposed holding interval. Posted `settle_funding` cash flows are signed account-currency amounts and may debit or credit cash.

Concentration limits are multiples of current equity. Portfolio stop-risk, daily loss and drawdown limits are fractions of equity. `correlation_group` is an explicit risk bucket, not estimated correlation or a claim that assets are statistically independent. The common test bucket is a synthetic conservative grouping. Opposite sides and correlated positions are counted gross; there is no offsetting or hedge credit. One active/reserved allocation per symbol is allowed. All limits are caller policy choices, not optimized defaults.

## Isolated M7-v1 boundary

The actual current interface is **M7-v1 SignalRecord**, not `SignalDecision`. `proposal_from_m7(record, *, as_of, events=())` requires a typed, available, QUALIFIED record with `qualification_passed=True`, a signal ID and consistent LONG/SHORT side. It preserves source signal/decision/pattern/configuration/input identities, availability, expiry and invalidation level. M7 score, coverage and confidence never determine quantity or risk eligibility. The market mark used for sizing is separately supplied; the M7 confirmation close is retained only as the source reference.

Available linked INVALIDATED/EXPIRED events mark the proposal terminal. Future events are excluded from historical assembly. `RiskEngine.observe_m7_terminal(event, *, as_of)` records an immutable terminal receipt, cancels a matching unfilled reservation, and notes any already-open allocation. It never closes an open position or rewrites a prior risk decision. Terminal receipts retain both original event availability and M8 receipt/cutoff time. The caller must deliver the complete causal upstream lifecycle stream; an immutable QUALIFIED record alone cannot prove that no later terminal event exists. Missing or dishonest provenance cannot be authenticated by this adapter.

M7's independent review is complete with PASS, and the adapter targets its committed M7-v1 interface at `0e7c8b84ef540ad1c4774746ed84849893d8ddd4`. The adapter rejects unsupported schemas and nonqualified records. No M7 source, tests, documentation, scoring or lifecycle semantics are changed to accommodate M8. CURRENT is accepted only as an offline provenance mode; it does not enable live trading.

## Sizing methodology

Let `E` be current account equity, `r` the risk fraction, `m` contract multiplier and `f` the account/USDT FX rate. Per-trade budget is `max(0,E*r)`.

1. Round LONG stops down and SHORT stops up to the price tick, enlarging planned adverse distance. A stop must remain strictly protective relative to the current market mark.
2. Apply adverse entry slippage and tick rounding (LONG up, SHORT down). Apply adverse stop slippage and tick rounding (LONG down, SHORT up). This produces modeled entry `Pe` and stop execution `Ps`.
3. Per-contract planned loss is `direction*(Pe-Ps)*m*f + Pe*m*f*entry_fee_rate + Ps*m*f*exit_fee_rate + max(Pe,Ps)*m*f*funding_debit_rate`.
4. Slippage and adverse rounding are already in the modeled prices; the separately reported slippage estimate is explanatory and is **not added a second time**. Rounding the raw stop toward the adverse tick is included in price loss.
5. Per-contract margin is `Pe*m*f/leverage`. Immediate capital reservation is margin plus entry fee plus adverse entry mark-to-fill loss. Future exit fees and funding are reserved as planned stop risk, not charged immediately as realized costs.
6. Bound quantity by per-trade risk, available capital, gross exposure, symbol concentration, correlation-bucket concentration, aggregate stop risk, daily loss headroom and drawdown headroom. An optional requested quantity adds another ceiling.
7. Always round quantity **down** to the quantity step. Reject if zero, below minimum quantity, or below quote-currency minimum notional. Never round up or increase the budget to satisfy a minimum. Recheck the final planned loss and reserved capital against their hard budgets.

Daily headroom is current equity minus the day's allowed floor minus existing aggregate stop risk. Drawdown headroom is current equity minus the allowed peak-equity floor minus existing aggregate stop risk. These conservative overlays may reduce quantity below the 10% per-trade ceiling. Missing or stale marks/FX prevent sizing rather than becoming zeros. Leverage changes capital capacity, not the per-trade risk budget.

`APPROVED` means the requested grid-valid size, or the risk-only maximum grid size when no quantity was requested, fits every cap. `REDUCED` means another cap or requested-quantity precision reduced that size. `REJECTED` covers unsupported proposal state, stale/missing evidence, invalid stop, leverage limits or unaffordable minimums. `BLOCKED` covers portfolio halts, slot/symbol restrictions and consumed signal/formation identities. Malformed types, future inputs, revised observations and out-of-order events raise before state changes.

## Accounting, reservations and portfolio limits

`Portfolio(as_of=..., cash=2000, currency='INR')` starts a new offline journal. `observe(quote, kind='MARK'|'FX', as_of=..., event_id=...)` admits only available, non-revised, chronological observations. Newer observations may change marks; same-observation-time revisions are rejected. `snapshot(as_of=...)` cannot query before the current replay cursor. Historical snapshots are retained in immutable journal events.

Cash includes realized price P&L, entry/exit fees and posted funding. Equity = cash + unrealized linear P&L at the latest known mark and FX. Position notional is not debited. Used entry margin is locked account capital; available capital = equity - used margin - pending reservations. FX changes revalue unrealized quote-currency P&L and current notional; entry fee and locked account margin retain their booking amounts. This is a single-account linear model, not an exchange collateral/margin emulator.

Open-position stop risk is remaining mark-to-stop loss plus estimated exit fee and remaining horizon funding debit, floored at zero. Already-paid entry costs are sunk in equity. Posted funding debits reduce the remaining funding estimate down to zero; credits do not erase prior debits or turn expected future costs into a credit. Pending reservations retain their full pre-entry planned loss. This avoids counting paid costs again while preserving conservative aggregate exposure.

If a mark already crosses the modeled stop, risk valuation uses the worse of the original stop fill and an adverse-slippage/tick-rounded market exit. It never assumes a favorable but unreachable old stop execution, or lets that apparent price improvement erase remaining fees/funding. This is a valuation allowance only; it does not simulate an automatic exit. Past mark-to-market losses are already included in equity.

Each approved decision atomically creates one private `Allocation`. Risk decisions and terminal receipts are append-only immutable histories. Each adjudicated signal and `(symbol, formation_id)` is consumed, including rejected/blocked proposals; later equity or policy evidence does not implicitly retry it. Identical repeated evaluation calls return the original decision without creating a second reservation. Canceling or closing cannot release formation consumption. Cross-M7-policy signal IDs cannot retry the same M3 formation within a risk actor.

A Portfolio admits one RiskEngine owner for its lifetime, preventing another actor from resetting consumption/cooldown policy over the same live in-memory book. Use a fresh portfolio and replay original inputs to compare another policy. Processing is sequential; concurrent writers and multi-process/durable deduplication are not implemented. Caller-owned internal dataclasses and private journal primitives are trusted in-process objects, not validated external wire payloads.

For offline booking use **RiskEngine.simulate_open**: it rechecks current halts, capital, concentration, stop-risk headroom and freshness before invoking the accounting journal. Portfolio's lower-level `simulate_open` additionally rejects expired allocations, stale source deadlines, changed mark/FX identities, insufficient current per-trade equity and already-consumed reservations. Direct Portfolio methods are trusted journal primitives for synthetic accounting, not an alternative risk-approval API. An allocation is not repriced if quotes change: cancel it; no implicit same-formation retry is introduced. `Portfolio.cancel` explicitly releases pending capital. Expired reservations remain conservatively reserved until explicitly canceled or a terminal event is delivered; they cannot be opened after expiry.

`Portfolio.simulate_close` books one complete synthetic exit fill with slippage already included once, using explicit exit fee rate and the currently known FX. Partial exits, automatic stop triggering, order fills and liquidation execution are not implemented. `settle_funding` posts explicit signed account cash flows. New entry fees immediately reduce equity; posted funding and every accounting event update loss metrics. All computed cash/accounting updates are validated on a detached draft before mutation.

UTC daily baseline uses the prior event's equity at a new day; opening gaps are not erased by resetting to post-gap equity. The day's minimum equity latches the daily loss halt until the next UTC day, even after recovery. Maximum portfolio drawdown is measured from event-sampled peak equity and remains latched for the replay. Completed-trade net P&L includes entry fees, exit fees and all posted funding. A negative net close increments consecutive losses; a nonnegative close resets the streak. Reaching the configured loss count blocks until `last_loss_at + cooldown_seconds`; exact deadline permits a new formation. The streak itself is retained after cooldown, so a subsequent loss restarts the cooldown.

## Causality, determinism and output

Every mark/FX has observation and receipt timestamps; assumptions/specifications have explicit availability. Future inputs fail without consuming signals or mutating state. External publication authenticity and missing historical observations remain caller responsibilities. Future M7 terminal events cannot affect past proposals. Portfolio events and risk decisions enforce chronological cursors; revised input under an existing event ID fails, while exact replay duplicates are idempotent. A fresh observation exactly at its configured age deadline remains usable; one second later is stale. Reservations preserve source identities and the earliest quote/FX/spec freshness deadline. Their signal expiry cannot be extended by M8.

Arithmetic uses a local 40-digit Decimal context and never changes ambient settings. Numeric wire values are canonical decimal strings, timestamps ISO UTC, tuples arrays. `RiskDecision.to_dict()` returns M8-v1 with decision/status/reasons, decision availability, signal/configuration/portfolio/input IDs, current equity, risk budget, quantity, modeled entry/stop, account notional, margin, reserved capital, planned loss, estimated fees/slippage/funding, allocation ID and the complete immutable proposal/spec/cost/leverage/request/predecision portfolio inputs. Confidence/profitability is not inferred. Instrument/cost identity is in the immutable input hash, not silently folded into an assumed global default.

`Portfolio.history`, `RiskEngine.history` and `RiskEngine.terminal_history` expose immutable tuples. IDs hash canonical inputs, policies and predecision state. Replaying the same original ordered observations, lifecycle events, evaluations and synthetic bookkeeping commands recreates identical state and decisions. No durable restart loader or serialized-state restoration is provided; replay original inputs from portfolio initialization. Do not reconstruct past risk from today's marks or FX. No backtester, live or paper integration is enabled.
