Original files were copied byte-for-byte into snapshots/original and SHA256 compared before edits. Git baseline: 19d6f21. No historical datasets or reports existed in this checkout. No exchange connection or dependency installation was performed.

The implementation immediately before the independent-review fixes was also copied and hash-verified (12 files) into snapshots/m0-m2-before-review-fixes. Configuration is the typed strategy.Params dataclass, validated at run entry. Existing CLI capital/fee/slippage options remain available. Other explicit research settings can be supplied through Params in Python; no silent strategy parameter tuning was performed.

## Data and time

One linear perpetual instrument per run, account and prices in quote currency. Input timestamp is candle OPEN time in UTC; numeric timestamps are explicitly milliseconds. Candles must be contiguous 5m rows on boundaries. Duplicate timestamps, nonfinite OHLCV, invalid price bounds and negative volume fail validation. Optional observations are candle-close observations: callers must supply point-in-time values, not later revisions. Missing optional layers retain the original neutral baseline behavior; they are not evidence that those layers were tested.

Validation rejects bars ending after the current UTC time (or a supplied historical as_of cutoff) and rejects any explicitly false/missing/nonboolean is_closed flag when that column is supplied. An optional available_at column records candle availability. Optional feature availability uses <column>_available_at (for example open_interest_available_at). Non-null observations with supplied but missing/late availability timestamps are rejected. The deadline is candle close for predictors and candle open for funding_event_rate settlements. This simulator does not automatically realign delayed data: the caller must do that before supplying it. Without availability metadata, historical-at-close availability remains an explicit reported assumption, not proven provenance. Availability checks cannot verify whether a publisher's timestamp is truthful.

15m/1h/4h intervals are left-closed and right-labeled, and require all constituent candles. Decisions use base candle closes. A pending decision fills at the following candle open under an explicitly zero-latency approximation. No orders originate from future candle closes. Warmup data precedes --start. --end excludes candles not completed by that boundary. Same-bar entries receive stop protection.

## Execution/accounting

Entry and exit prices include adverse slippage exactly once. Each fill pays its own fee. Balance includes all realized gains/losses, fees and funding. Equity additionally includes remaining unrealized P&L. Complete trade P&L sums every exit and all associated costs. The initial equity sample and final force-close costs are included in metrics.

Stop-first applies when the same candle reaches stop and target; gap stops use the worse opening price. TP1 exits half the original quantity. After TP1 the original strategy's 0.1% protective stop adjustment remains. Its activation market price is the candle open when the open already crosses TP1, otherwise TP1 itself. If the adjusted stop is already triggered at activation, it exits at that activation market price with adverse slippage; it never fills at an unreachable trigger price. Otherwise, a same-bar adjusted-stop touch takes precedence over TP2. Target fills remain conservatively priced at the target plus adverse slippage, including favorable opening gaps. No trailing strategy was added. max_bars enforces duration. Known opening-gap fills carry opening timestamps; inferred intrabar fills carry candle-end timestamps and explicit sequence ordering because their exact times are unknowable. Applied adverse slippage may move a modeled fill outside an observed OHLC range; it cannot create favorable price improvement.

funding_rate remains a strategy observation, NOT an instruction to debit funding every candle. Optional funding_event_rate is a settlement event at that row's opening boundary, charged to an existing position using that opening price as the documented mark proxy. Positive rates debit LONG and credit SHORT. NaN means no event. New positions at that boundary are excluded. Without this column, funding is explicitly not tested. Precise exchange settlement marks and sub-bar settlements are outside this model.

An absent or entirely NaN settlement column is reported as not_tested. Reports include the number of supplied settlement observations in the evaluation window; even with events, coverage remains unverified because there is no authoritative settlement schedule. Settlement columns are excluded from signal feature input. Funding is processed before a simultaneous opening-gap stop, according to the existing boundary convention.

Quantity budgets include estimated entry/stop fees and adverse stop slippage. Gaps and funding can exceed planned risk. Leverage remains only a notional cap; liquidation and venue quantity/margin rules are not simulated.

For stops already triggered by entry slippage, sizing uses the opening market exit cost instead of assuming the stop trigger price is obtainable. Nonfinite/invalid prices, quantities, marks, configuration and accounting arithmetic are rejected before balance mutation. Fee/slippage fractions must be within [0, 1); that mathematical bound is not a statement about realistic venue costs. Default trading parameters have not been increased or tuned.

Daily and weekly equity loss latches reset independently at UTC day/ISO-week boundaries, using the last equity observation from the preceding period as their new baseline. Opening gaps are therefore attributed to the NEW period rather than erased by rebasing. A daily reset does not clear a weekly latch. Hard drawdown halt persists. Existing positions continue being managed.

Limits are now sampled at opening, funding, entry, partial/full exit, and closing events. Closing and inferred intrabar events are attributed to the bar's period using a risk timestamp one nanosecond before its closing boundary. The actual next opening event belongs to the new period. This is a deterministic historical convention; exact intrabar movements and unobserved equity excursions are not simulated. Halts and reported maximum drawdown consume the identical ordered event stream. Entry fees and slippage are observed immediately, even if prices subsequently recover.

## Outputs and checks

run retains the (summary, equity, trades) interface. Fill, funding, signal and risk_equity records are attached to equity.attrs and exported by the CLI. Existing output directories are rejected to prevent overwriting research. Summary includes all configuration and input SHA256. Empty evaluation windows are rejected. Empty trade/fill/funding CSVs retain schema headers. Automated tests use unittest and synthetic fixtures; they do not establish profitability.

The max_drawdown_pct field uses risk events; close_max_drawdown_pct separately describes the regular equity.csv series. Sharpe continues to use regular 5m returns, avoiding irregular event sampling. The legacy fees_slippage_model and pnl_pct fields are restored. pnl_pct is a fractional return (0.01 means 1%), measured against equity at entry and including ALL fills and funding. Trade, fill and order identifiers are deterministic within each run; every modeled fill is a separate single-fill order. These IDs do not imply real exchange order lifecycle simulation. Fills include remaining quantity and post-fill balance, and funding records link to trade_id.

Remaining work: empirical validation with recovered data, portfolio risk, exchange simulation details, provenance acquisition, and Freqtrade integration. The user's revised M3 candlestick-pattern engine and M4 news-engine requirements are future scope only. No indicators, pattern detectors or news integration were added. min_rr remains an existing unused hypothesis setting; no new strategy filter was introduced. Existing indicator formulas, score asymmetry and neutral missing-data behavior are preserved pending later approved work.
