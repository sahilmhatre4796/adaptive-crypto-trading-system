## Interface and timing

`StructureEngine(StructureConfig(...)).scan(raw, symbol, timeframe='15m', as_of=...)` returns an immutable `StructureResult`: configuration and stable configuration ID, instrument/timeframe, evaluation cutoff, last observed availability, confirmed swings, current zones, immutable events and current structural sequence direction. Every scan replays its input deterministically; no hidden mutable engine state or persistence is involved.

The engine reuses M3's completed-candle parser, OHLC validation, UTC timestamps, timeframe alignment, symbol checks, contiguous-bar checks and monotonic availability contract. Scope remains BTC/USDT and ETH/USDT on explicit 5m/15m/1h/4h inputs. Default responsibility remains 15m structure, 1h direction and 4h regime; this module does not infer either higher-timeframe service or resample silently. Missing availability metadata retains the inherited historical close-time assumption. Explicit unfinished flags are rejected. Future/unavailable candles contribute no evidence, although the complete supplied frame must pass validation.

`pivot_at` is a candle's OPEN timestamp. `price_confirmed_at` is the confirming candle's CLOSE. `confirmed_at` is its availability, which may be later. A level cannot be used on a candle that began before it became available. The breakout candle cannot retest itself; a later candle must also begin at or after breakout availability. These rules deliberately discard ambiguous price action during receipt delays. Appending valid future bars cannot rewrite earlier swings/events. To obtain past zone states, scan with that past `as_of`; the final zone list is a snapshot, not a historical state series.

## Explicit research definitions

These rules are independently implemented research definitions, not universal technical-analysis definitions or calibrated trading rules.

| Evidence | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed swing | Default two preceding and two following candles. A high must be strictly higher than every neighbor high; a low strictly lower than every neighbor low. Equal-price plateaus yield no pivot. An outside candle can be both a high and low without an arbitrary directional vote. |
| Swing relations | Compare each confirmed high to the preceding confirmed high: HH/LH/EH; each low to the preceding low: HL/LL/EL. The first of each kind is FIRST. Consecutive same-kind pivots remain evidence and are not retrospectively collapsed into a zigzag. |
| Structural direction | Latest high/low relations HH+HL give BULLISH; LH+LL give BEARISH. Other fully known pairs give RANGE; a missing/FIRST pair gives UNKNOWN. A changed sequence emits STRUCTURE_CHANGE only on confirmation. It is local structure, not the 4h regime service. |
| Zones | Every confirmed low creates SUPPORT from its low to low + 10% of that pivot candle's full range; a high creates RESISTANCE from high − 10% of range to high. Width is frozen from observed prices; it is not optimized or retroactively resized/merged. Default lifetime is 96 intervals after the confirmation candle's PRICE close, exclusive at expiry. Delayed receipt never renews lifetime. |
| Breakout | Close strictly beyond the outer level: above resistance upper edge or below support lower edge. Touches and equal closes do not qualify. Gaps can qualify by close but do not imply an actual fill. ACTIVE → BROKEN, once per zone. |
| BOS / CHOCH | A breakout in the existing confirmed BULLISH/BEARISH sequence direction is BOS; the opposite is CHOCH. UNKNOWN/RANGE gives UNCLASSIFIED. A CHOCH tag does not itself rewrite the confirmed swing sequence. |
| Retest | A separate later candle intersects the zone, stays beyond its far edge on its wick and closes beyond the breakout edge. For upward breaks: low >= lower, low <= upper and close > upper; downward is the mirror. A deeper wick and favorable reclaim is ambiguous and does not confirm. BROKEN → RETESTED; the zone then supplies the opposite role for location evidence. |
| Retest window | Default eight intervals after the breakout candle's PRICE close, inclusive of that final close. Receipt must also be within the window; a one-bar window admits the next timely candle. A BROKEN zone expires after this deadline or at its original zone expiry, whichever comes first. RETESTED zones retain their original lifetime. |
| Failed break | A later close through the opposite edge of a BROKEN or RETESTED zone: upward break closing below lower, downward closing above upper. Failure takes precedence over retest and ends the zone as FAILED. A close inside the zone or equal to an edge is unresolved. |
| Sweep | An ACTIVE resistance zone's upper edge is strictly exceeded by a wick but the candle closes below its lower edge; support is mirrored. Emit at most one SWEEP per zone, keeping it ACTIVE for a possible later breakout. This describes OHLC geometry at a confirmed structural level, not observed liquidations or proof of resting liquidity. It does not inspect inside bars or duplicate M3 Hikkake. |

Expired and failed zones never reactivate. Distinct zones can overlap and generate correlated evidence; M7 must group it rather than count each as an independent vote. Event IDs include algorithm/configuration, instrument, timeframe, event kind, level identity and candle. Repeated replay produces identical IDs. Revised historical input is a different replay; this is not a durable revision/restart or order-deduplication store.

Expired/terminal zones are retained in the result for provenance but removed from the active replay loop. Active work is bounded by the configured lifetime rather than all historical zones. Results still retain the full swings/events/zone history in memory.

## M3 location interface

```python
from trading.structure import StructureEngine
from trading.pattern_context import ContextSnapshot

result = StructureEngine().scan(completed_15m, 'BTC/USDT', as_of=decision_at)
distances = result.level_distances(price=current_price, atr=current_atr)
snapshot = ContextSnapshot(
    symbol=result.symbol, available_at=decision_at, atr=current_atr, **distances
)
```

`level_distances` returns the nearest eligible zone-edge distances in ATR units, zero inside a zone, and `None` where no level exists. ACTIVE support must be below/containing price and resistance above/containing price. Only RETESTED zones reverse role; unresolved BROKEN zones contribute no distance. Failed/expired zones contribute none. The caller supplies contemporaneous positive price/ATR, chooses the correct historical `as_of` and owns freshness of that price and any 1h/4h context. The example retains UNKNOWN higher direction/regime: location alone cannot validate M3 or approve a trade. No indicator, news, risk or strategy formula was changed.
