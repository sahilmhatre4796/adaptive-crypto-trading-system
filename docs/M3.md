# M3 - Candlestick pattern engine

Implementation scope: six deterministic OHLC pattern families, neutral doji evidence, separate context assessment, timestamped immutable events, an independent 5m setup lifecycle, and an offline evidence exporter. No baseline strategy, indicator, accounting, execution, funding or risk formula was changed. This milestone does not enable trading or establish profitability.

## Files and interfaces

- trading/patterns.py: validated immutable OHLC values, geometry measurements, PatternConfig, pure harami/hikkake/engulfing/wick_reversals/stars/doji functions and detect_geometry.
- trading/pattern_context.py: causal preceding-direction classification, timestamped ContextSnapshot and independent ContextAssessment.
- trading/pattern_engine.py: PatternEngine.scan, completed-bar validation/resampling, immutable PatternEvent, evaluate_setup and SetupTracker.
- scan_patterns.py: offline CSV-to-JSON export with configuration, source/context hashes and a reproducible as_of cutoff. It has no exchange or order path.
- tests/test_patterns_geometry.py and tests/test_pattern_engine.py: geometry, context, lifecycle, export and causality checks.

M0-M2 source files and existing tests remain unchanged. The original snapshot and the 11 Phase A source/document hashes are checked separately. Existing 36 tests remain required alongside new M3 tests.

## Exact research definitions

For each candle, body = abs(close-open), range = high-low, body_low/high = min/max(open,close), lower wick = body_low-low, upper wick = high-body_high. Ratios are dimensionless. OHLC values must be finite, positive and internally consistent; zero-range candles are accepted as data but produce no pattern. Body position is the real-body midpoint's position within the candle range.

These are explicit configurable research definitions, not a claim of exact equivalence to TA-Lib, a universal textbook definition, or independently calibrated trading rules. Thresholds and score weights have not been optimized against market data.

| Family | Definition and default boundaries |
| --- | --- |
| Harami | Opposite-colored, non-doji bodies; first body >=50% of its own range; second body <=60% of first body and contained within it, with 2% of first-body tolerance at either boundary. Same-color harami is deliberately excluded by this stricter variant. Bullish second body follows a bearish first body, and vice versa. |
| Engulfing | Opposite-colored, non-doji bodies; second body at least as large as first, containing its real-body endpoints within 2% first-body tolerance. Body expansion threshold is configurable. Wick engulfment is not required. |
| Hammer / Hanging Man | Shared lower-wick geometry: body >10% and <=35% of range, lower wick >=2 bodies, upper wick <=10% of range. Preceding decline names Hammer/BULLISH; preceding advance names Hanging Man/BEARISH. Missing or sideways prior direction leaves neutral lower_wick evidence. Body color is not required. |
| Shooting Star / Inverted Hammer | Shared upper-wick mirror geometry. Prior advance names Shooting Star/BEARISH; prior decline names Inverted Hammer/BULLISH. Missing or sideways direction leaves neutral upper_wick evidence. |
| Morning / Evening Star | Three candles: first directional body >=50% of its range, middle body <=30% of first body and near the first closing extreme, third opposite body >=50% of its own range and close penetrating >=50% of first body. The middle body must remain within 25% of first body from that closing extreme; third starts in the reversal half and closes beyond the middle real body. No overnight gap is required. The middle candle may be a doji. |
| Hikkake | Strict full-range inside bar (lower high AND higher low than mother). False downward break requires the next candle to have lower high and lower low than the inside bar; false upward break is the mirror. Confirmation is the FIRST close beyond the opposite inside-bar boundary within the next 3 candles. A mere wick touch or equal close does not confirm. Extension beyond the false-break extreme cancels first, even if that same candle would confirm. A two-sided outside break is ambiguous and excluded. The false-break extreme is the invalidation price. |
| Doji (context only) | Real body <=10% of a positive range. Always NEUTRAL/INDECISION. It cannot independently confirm a directional setup, regardless of external context. |

Hikkake is an inside-bar failure detector, not a market-structure liquidity-sweep engine. Multiple distinct inside-bar formations may coexist, but the same formation confirms only once. The close-only confirmation and extreme-extension cancellation are deliberately conservative variants and must be included in later parameter/definition sensitivity research.

Concept references inspected: [TA-Lib Hikkake](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLHIKKAKE.c), [Harami](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLHARAMI.c), [Engulfing](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLENGULFING.c). No TA-Lib installation, dependency or copied source implementation was added.

## Context and strength

Geometry functions accept OHLC only and do not infer trend. Context validation is separate:

1. Preceding trend uses three candles BEFORE the formation starts, normalized by their median range. Net close movement of at least +/-0.5 such ranges gives bullish/bearish; otherwise neutral. Insufficient/zero-scale history is unknown. These values and lookback are configurable.
2. Harami, Engulfing and Star formations require preceding reversal context. Wick names/classification use that same prior history. Hikkake does not require an opposing preceding trend; its confirmed inside-bar failure provides its local context.
3. A ContextSnapshot carries the asset, availability timestamp, 1H direction and its candle close, 4H regime and its candle close, optional ATR, relative volume and supplied support/resistance distances in ATR units. Known higher-timeframe data must identify aligned completed candles and cannot precede their close.
4. Direction/regime must be known, non-conflicting and not abnormal. Unknown/uncertain data earns no support. 1H and 4H evidence older than their respective intervals is marked stale; the overall snapshot must be at most 15 minutes old by default.
5. A meaningful supplied level is required: bullish support or bearish resistance within one ATR by default. M3 does NOT invent these levels or implement M5 early. If none is supplied, status is INSUFFICIENT and the 5m gate cannot confirm a directional setup.
6. Valid supplied relative volume below 1.0 rejects context; missing volume supplies no bonus and is not mandatory. Pattern range is checked against supplied ATR, otherwise against the preceding OHLC range scale where available (default ratio 0.1 to 3). This is a scale filter, not a new ATR implementation.

Context status is VALIDATED, REJECTED, INSUFFICIENT or, for doji, INDECISION. Supporting/conflicting/missing reasons and the actual snapshot are retained. Pattern strength is a bounded ranking: 65% of geometry score plus small explicit bonuses for prior context, higher direction, regime, location and volume; conflicts reduce it. Missing groups are not redistributed into favorable votes. Neither strength nor geometry score is a success probability. Later M7 must avoid counting correlated pattern evidence repeatedly.

## Time and event contracts

Default responsibility remains 4H regime / 1H bias / 15M patterns / 5M entry confirmation. The engine can scan explicit 5m/15m/1h/4h OHLC inputs on their own timeframe; it never relabels a pattern onto a different interval.

Input timestamp means OPEN time. Numeric timestamps mean epoch milliseconds; naive textual timestamps are interpreted as UTC. Timeframe boundaries, duplicate timestamps, gaps, malformed OHLC and explicitly unfinished flags are checked. Missing is_closed means the caller asserts completed historical input; the as_of gate still excludes candles not yet closed/available. An explicit false or missing flag in a supplied flag column is rejected.

Availability defaults to close time under the inherited zero-latency research assumption. Supplied availability must not precede close; only candles available by as_of are scanned. Availability must be nondecreasing with candle order, so a late missing candle cannot be silently inserted into an already-evaluated sequence. Normalize out-of-order arrivals upstream. The 5m resampling helper reuses M0-M2 validation and emits only complete bins without calculating indicators.

Each PatternEvent records symbol/timeframe, family/name/classification, detection and confirmation availability times, price-confirmation close time, opening-time candle references, measured candle geometry, score, context and snapshot, exact configuration, stable configuration/event IDs, invalidation price/condition and expiry. Events are frozen dataclasses. Hikkake's detection timestamp identifies the completed false-break candle; the event is emitted only when its later confirmation is available. It is never emitted retrospectively at its detection time.

Expiry is four pattern intervals after PRICE confirmation, not receipt. A delayed event may arrive already expired and cannot be refreshed by late receipt. Future context snapshots are never selected to score an older event. Appending future bars or context does not rewrite earlier events. Revised source data with the same formation ID cannot overwrite a registered event in SetupTracker.

## Independent 5m lifecycle

evaluate_setup takes an event, a ClosedCandle with explicit matching asset, an as_of decision time and optional fresh context. It returns evidence, never a trade/order/risk approval. Rules:

- Candle must be completed, available and from the same asset; context configuration cannot be silently overridden.
- Candle must BEGIN at or after pattern availability. The final 5m sub-candle of a 15m formation cannot be reused as later entry confirmation.
- A stale 5m candle (at least one interval old) cannot backdate a confirmation. Expiry uses current evaluation time.
- Touching the pattern's invalidation extreme takes precedence over a favorable close. Expired or neutral events cannot confirm.
- Reassess context at the 5m decision time; it does not mutate the original 15m event. Missing/conflicting context leaves the setup waiting for a future valid bar or expiry.
- Bullish confirmation requires a bullish 5m body and close above the pattern's confirmation close; bearish is the mirror. This is a local price-action check, not duplicated support/resistance breakout logic.

SetupTracker enforces chronological observations, one processing per event/candle and terminal CONFIRMED/INVALIDATED/EXPIRED/REJECTED states. Repeat processing returns neutral DUPLICATE evidence. Its state is in-memory for research; durable restart recovery and portfolio-level duplicate-order prevention remain later M8/M9 work.

## Usage

Pure geometry:

```python
from trading.patterns import OHLC, detect_geometry
matches = detect_geometry([OHLC(105,106,94,95), OHLC(97,100,96,99)])
```

Offline 15m evidence export:

```powershell
python -B scan_patterns.py --csv data/BTCUSDT_15m.csv --symbol BTC/USDT --out runs/pattern-evidence-btc
```

Or completed 5m OHLCV input resampled to 15m:

```powershell
python -B scan_patterns.py --csv data/BTCUSDT_5m.csv --symbol BTC/USDT --input-timeframe 5m --timeframe 15m --out runs/pattern-evidence-btc
```

These commands require user-supplied files; no historical dataset was fabricated. Optional --config-json accepts geometry/context objects matching the frozen dataclasses. Optional --contexts-json accepts an ordered array of timestamped ContextSnapshot objects. Optional --as-of fixes the historical availability cutoff. Context/source hashes, exact configuration and the effective cutoff are exported. Existing output directories are rejected. Without context, geometry evidence is still exportable but cannot pass the independent context/5m gate.

Outputs are patterns.json and summary.json. Use dataclasses.asdict for API serialization. No new dependency, baseline strategy import, exchange adapter, Telegram service, model request or live/paper order loop was added.
