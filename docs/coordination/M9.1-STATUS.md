## Approval and safety boundary

Only exact decisions registered by the connected M8 actor can be admitted. APPROVED/REDUCED additionally require an active matching reservation and eligible original source data/expiry; REJECTED/BLOCKED produce DENIED receipts with no order. M7 qualification, dictionaries, altered decisions, unknown histories and standalone fills cannot bypass that gate. Entry booking always calls M8 `simulate_open` for current risk rechecks and retains the exact approved quantity/model. Tests exercise these paths explicitly.

Available M7 terminal evidence delivered through either API revokes a pending intent. Future terminal evidence cannot alter a past decision. Open positions receive a notice only; exit bookkeeping requires a separate explicit synthetic fill. External opens/closes are unsupported and conflicting state fails loudly. Exact repeated callbacks cannot rebook or reopen; revised callbacks, unsupported partial/price-drift fills and invalid transitions raise before local booking.

All M8 fees, costs, contract rules and FX remain explicit. INR equity, USDT stake and contract/base units are distinguished. The user-selected experimental 10% current-equity risk policy is unchanged.
